This list is dedicated to the presentation of essays, monographs,
reviews, abstracts and details of current research, etc. Posts are
expected to be scholarly, academic, or at least well thought-out
within the frame work of Extropian principles (see Extropy Number 9
for the latest the Extropian Principles V 2.0.) Original research is
especially welcome.

This list was created to provide a forum for material that has either
not been appearing on our main list or which has been trapped in the
noise. If you wish to join in the free ranging discussion of Extropian
ideas, subscribe to the main list by sending a note to:
        extropians-request@gnu.ai.mit.edu

This list is considered private. That means that we have a number of
rules about how you may use material you get from your subscription to
the list, and how you interact with the list. Your continued
subscription indicates your acceptance of these rules. In Extropian
terms, this list is governed by a poly-centric legal code. While the
having such detailed rules may seem odd or even strange to you, 
the code/rules are used here for two (2) reasons.

1a) Posts may be essays, monographs, reviews, abstracts and details of
current research, etc. Posts are expected to be scholarly, academic,
or at least well thought-out within the frame work of Extropian
principles.

2). The copyright on each post is held by the poster.

2a.) Cross-posting, forwarding, etc. is not allowed. Other ExI lists
excepted unless prohibited by the poster. (It is ok to make a quoted
reply to the main list unless the poster prohibits it.)

3). All posts will also appear on a private conf. on The Well.
        (Send mail to habs@well.sf.ca.us for admission.)

4). Posters may indicate alternative copyright restrictions. For
example: A poster may indicate a post is copyleft, etc.

5). Any post in which the poster has indicated by the alternative
copyright that it may be cross-posted will be stored at a public
accessed FTP site. (This is still being planned).

6). No discussion or quoted replies are allowed on the exi-essay list.
You may write detailed response to an article, but it must meet the
criteria of this list. Such a reponse must be able to stand alone/up
without the prior work. For example, if you don't agree with someone's
research, conduct your own and report on it/write it up for this list.

(If it is not a well written article of fact please post it somewhere
else.)

6b) A regular administrative post will be made by the list manager.
It will appear every few weeks and also when needed by special
activities or offerings.

7) Violations of these rules will result in the removal of the
offender from the list for a time period set by the list manager. Your
continued subscription to the exi-essay list is your agreement to
abide by these rules.
