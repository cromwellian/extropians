This is a list for the presenation of well written articles of fact.

This list was created to provide a forum for material that has either
not been appearing on our main list or which has been trapped in the
noise. If the level is not keep high, I will stop the list and have
Perry add moderation code.

When you see and read an article that interests you, you should address
your comments to the poster!!! You will have to create a new message or
adjust your mail headers. If on your mail reader you simply hit the
reply (key/button/etc.) our software will send the reply to the
*main* list (extropians@gnu.ai.mit.edu). Please be careful about this
as some posters may forbid cross-posting to the main list.

4). Any post that in which the poster has indicated by the alternative
copyright that it may be cross-posted will be stored at a public accessed
FTP site. (Contact derek@cs.wisc.edu, contractor for this service.)

5). No discussion or quoted replies are allowed on the exi-essay list.

It will appear every few weeks and also when needed by special activities
or offerings.

6) Violations of these rules will result in the removal of the offender
from the list for a time period set by the list manager. Your continued
subscription to the exi-essay list is your agreement to abide by these
rules.
